🎯 Why the "Linear Slate" Direction Wins

Inspired by Linear, Raycast, and Vercel. Dark surfaces with 1px zinc borders reduce eye fatigue, convey instant engineering credibility, and allow colorful brand logos (React, Next.js, Stripe) to stand out cleanly.
